PACK 1

Username Prompts

3 prompts

Page ka naam hi pehli impression hai. Ye teeno prompts milke tumhe short, catchy aur brandable usernames nikaal ke denge.

PROMPT 115 brandable usernames — niche + audience

You are an Instagram branding expert. I want to start an Instagram page in the [your niche] niche, targeting [your target audience]. Suggest 15 short, catchy, brandable usernames. Rules: avoid random numbers, keep most under 15 characters, make them easy to say out loud and easy to spell, and choose names that will still make sense if the page grows into a bigger brand. Present them as a table with three columns: Username, Why it works (one line), and Vibe (fun / expert / premium). End by highlighting your top 3 picks with a one-line reason each.

PROMPT 2Naam + niche mix — 10 usernames

You are a personal branding strategist. My real name is [your name] and my content will be about [your niche]. Suggest 10 usernames that mix my name with my niche in a professional but memorable way — founder-style, not a random page. Avoid numbers, hyphens and misspellings that look unprofessional. For each username, add a one-line note on the impression it creates. At the end, mark your top 3 picks and explain in one line each why they are the strongest.

PROMPT 3Expert personal brand — 10 usernames

You are a personal brand consultant. I am building a personal brand page around [your niche/skill], where I want to be seen as an expert, not just a random page. Suggest 10 Instagram usernames that are clean, easy to spell and type, and instantly signal authority in my niche. Avoid overused filler words like "official", "hub" or "gram" unless they genuinely fit. Rank all 10 from most to least authoritative, with a one-line reason for each.

PACK 2

Bio Prompts

2 prompts

Profile pe aane ke baad bio hi decide karti hai ki follow hoga ya nahi. Dono prompts ready-made options nikaal ke denge.

PROMPT 15 bio options — hook + value + CTA

You are an Instagram copywriter. Write 5 different Instagram bio options for a [your niche] page. Each bio must have: (1) a one-line hook that stops the scroll, (2) a clear statement of the value I provide, and (3) a short call-to-action to follow or DM. Keep each bio under 150 characters and use 2-3 relevant emojis. After the 5 options, pick the strongest one and explain in one line why it converts best.

PROMPT 2Tone-pick bio — 3-4 lines + link CTA

You are an Instagram bio specialist. Write an Instagram bio for a [your niche] page in a [fun / professional / motivational — pick one] tone. Structure it as 3-4 short lines with line breaks: line 1 = hook, line 2 = who I help and how, line 3 = proof or personality, final line = a CTA to check the link in bio. Also give me 2 alternative versions of line 1 (the hook) so I can test which one performs better.

PACK 3

Content Idea Prompts — 30-Day Calendar

3 prompts

"Kal kya post karun?" ka confusion khatam. Ek prompt se poore 30 din ka calendar, reel ideas aur carousel ideas — sab ready.

PROMPT 1Poora 30-day content calendar

You are an Instagram content strategist. Create a 30-day Instagram content calendar for a [your niche] page targeting [your target audience]. Mix reels, carousels, and static posts in a healthy ratio (roughly 50% reels, 30% carousels, 20% static). For each day give only: day number, format (reel / carousel / static), and a one-line topic idea — no scripts. Balance educational, relatable, trend-based and selling content across the month, and mark your 5 strongest days with a star so I know what to prioritize.

PROMPT 215 beginner-friendly reel ideas

You are a short-form video strategist. Suggest 15 reel topic ideas for a [your niche] page that a complete beginner can film with just a phone in under 15 minutes each. Each idea should be likely to be saved or shared, not just liked. For every idea give three things: the one-line topic, why it gets saved or shared, and what the first 2 seconds of the reel should show so people stop scrolling.

PROMPT 310 carousel ideas — slide by slide

You are an Instagram carousel expert. Suggest 10 carousel post ideas for a [your niche] page. For each idea give: a scroll-stopping title, and a one-line description of what each slide should cover (slide 1 = hook, last slide = CTA). Prioritize carousels valuable enough to save, and for each one, call out the single line on slide 1 that will make people swipe.

PACK 4

Posting Strategy Prompt

1 prompt

Sirf content kaafi nahi — timing aur consistency bhi chahiye. Ye prompt tumhari weekly schedule bana dega, burnout ke bina.

PROMPT 1Weekly schedule — grow without burnout

You are an Instagram growth coach. I run a [your niche] Instagram page with [current follower count] followers, and I can realistically give [hours available per week] hours per week. Suggest a weekly posting schedule — which days, what time of day, and how many reels vs carousels vs static posts — so I grow consistently without burning out. Also include a 30-minute weekly batch-prep routine, and tell me exactly what to do if I miss a posting day.
